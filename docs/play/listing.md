## Full description (4000 max)

Vaultesque keeps your passwords, cards and private notes in an encrypted vault that
never leaves your phone. No account to create, no server to trust, no subscription.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is encrypted on your device with AES-256 (SQLCipher)
• Your master password is stretched with Argon2id, the modern standard for password hashing
• No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no tracking
• Works fully offline. The only network request is an optional, off-by-default breach check

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Logins, payment cards, bank accounts, secure notes, Wi-Fi, identities, passports and documents
• Custom fields, tags, favourites, archive and trash
• Encrypted file attachments up to 5 MB
• Built-in one-time codes (TOTP) for two-factor sign-in
• Fast search across your whole vault

AUTOFILL
• Fill logins in apps and browsers with Android Autofill
• Apps are matched by their signing key, never by name, so a look-alike app cannot trick it
• Save new logins as you sign up

PASSWORD HEALTH
• A health score for your vault
• Finds weak, reused and breached passwords, insecure websites and missing 2FA
• Checks against a million known breached passwords entirely on your phone
• Optional Have I Been Pwned check that sends only a 5-character hash prefix

GENERATOR
• Random passwords, passphrases and PINs with exact entropy shown
• Avoid look-alike characters, require every character type

LOCKED DOWN
• Unlock with fingerprint or face (optional)
• Auto-lock when you leave the app, turn off the screen, or stop using it
• Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked by default
• Clipboard is cleared automatically after 30 seconds
• Optional erase after too many failed unlock attempts

MOVE IN AND BACK UP
• Import from Enpass (JSON export)
• Export an encrypted .vault backup protected by its own password, and restore it on any phone

There is no password reset. Only you can open your vault, which also means only an
exported backup can recover it if you forget your master password.
